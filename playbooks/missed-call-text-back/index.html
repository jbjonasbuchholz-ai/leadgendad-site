Missed-call text-back

Every time you miss a call, the caller gets a text from you within seconds. They stay in your conversation instead of calling the next person on Google.

Build time45 min
Extra cost$0*
DifficultyEasy
Runs24/7

Why this matters

Most people who reach your voicemail don't leave a message. They hang up and call someone else. A text that arrives seconds after the missed call keeps the conversation going while you're with a client, driving, or at your kid's game.

You set it up once and it works on every missed call from then on.

What you need

  • A HighLevel account with a phone number in it. This is the number you give out and forward your calls to.
  • Texting approval for that number. In the US, carriers require business texting registration (called A2P 10DLC) before your texts will deliver reliably.
  • A booking link (optional). A HighLevel calendar link works well.
Do this first: if your number isn't registered for texting yet, start the registration in HighLevel today. Approval can take several days, and unregistered texts often get blocked.

*$0 extra if you already pay for HighLevel. Standard texting fees still apply.

The build

  1. Get your number ready to text

    5 min

    In HighLevel, open your phone number settings and confirm you have a number and that texting registration is approved or in progress.

    Forward your business calls to this number, or make it the number on your cards, ads, and Google profile.

  2. Write the auto-reply message

    10 min

    Keep it short, human, and end with a question so they reply. Use one of the scripts below and change the bracketed parts.

  3. Build the missed-call workflow

    15 min
    • Go to Automation and create a new workflow from scratch.
    • Add the trigger for call status, and filter it to missed or unanswered inbound calls.
    • Add a Send SMS action and paste in your message.
    • Save and turn the workflow on.

  4. Tag the lead + notify your phone

    10 min
    • Add an Add Tag action with the tag missed-call so you can find these leads later.
    • Add an internal notification to yourself so you see the missed call on your phone.

  5. Call yourself and test it

    5 min

    From a different phone, call your number and let it ring out. Check each item off below.

Copy-paste scripts

Real estate agent

Hey, it's [Your Name] with [Brokerage]. Sorry I missed your call, I'm with a client right now. Are you looking to buy, sell, or something else? You can also grab a time on my calendar here: [booking link]

Any local business

Hi, this is [Your Name] at [Business]. Sorry we missed your call! What can we help you with today? Reply here and we'll get right back to you.

After hours

Thanks for calling [Business]! We're closed right now, but I'll reach out first thing tomorrow. What's the best way I can help? Feel free to reply with a few details.

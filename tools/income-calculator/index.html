How the math works

  1. Income needed. Your monthly goal × 12. If you enter business expenses, the calculator grosses that up so you still take home your goal.
  2. What you keep per closing. Sale price × your commission × your split.
  3. Closings. Income needed ÷ what you keep per closing, rounded up.
  4. Leads. Each closing needs 1 ÷ (talk rate × booking rate × close rate) leads. Raise any of the three and the lead count drops.
